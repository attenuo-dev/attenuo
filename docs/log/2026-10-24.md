### 05:30 UTC: output labelling design note

Branch `docs/output-labelling-design`, docs only. New [design/output-labelling.md](../design/output-labelling.md) and [ADR-0009](../decisions/0009-output-labelling.md) (proposed) for the board item "Output labelling". The proxy would record every upstream result as an untrusted label with provenance (`tool_result` audit record, keyed by `chain_id`), Cedar would see `resource.destructive`, `context.session.tainted`, `tainted_by`, `args_contain_untrusted` and `context.approval`, and a forbid annotated `@obligation("approval")` would ask for a single-use approval bound to the call hash and the lease instead of refusing. Three phases: session taint (no agent change), argument-span matching, SDK labelled values.

- Problem cases: `workspace-06`, `travel-02`, `t-workspace-05`.
- False positives, counted by reading the cases (no run): session taint would ask on 18 of 66 allowed dev benign calls and 2 of 26 test (the test cases mostly omit the read their task implies); argument matching on about 10 dev (up to 12) and 1 test. Session taint gates 9 of the 10 dev misses; only `github-07`, a read, is outside it.
- Finding: exempting calls whose destination is pinned by the token would cut 6 of the 18 dev asks but also exempt nearly every class N attack, whose destination is right by construction. The pin exemption belongs on matched values, not on whole calls.
- Sources: CaMeL, FIDES, the f-secure LLM system, Progent, Beurer-Kellner et al.'s design patterns, Denning, Asbestos, Flume, winnowing, the MCP spec and its tool-annotations post, OpenAI's agent safety guide, Anthropic's browser prompt-injection report, AuthZEN 1.0. Links in the design note.

### 06:50 UTC: output labelling phase 1 (`feat/output-labels-phase1`)

- Built phase 1 of the output-labelling design without the approval store or the `/approvals` endpoints: catalog `destructive` and `output`, `[policy] import_annotations` (`never` by default, `narrowing` can only restrict), `tool_result` audit records for every allowed mediated answer (JSON and SSE, upstream errors included), run taint keyed by `chain_id`, the Cedar attributes of design section 3.6, `@obligation("approval")` giving `approval_required` and JSON-RPC `-32008` with `call_hash`, and `labels.tainted_by` on decision records. Example policies with tests in `config/policies/output-labels.cedar`.
- Choices where the design left room or did not quite work as written:
  - `approval_required` also needs a permit that would allow the call once the approval forbids are set aside. Without this, a call no permit allows would ask for an approval that could never help.
  - A `tool_result` record has decision `allow`, reason `tool_result`, and the call's `arguments_digest`. The digest covers the answer's `result` member, or its `error` member (`error: true`).
  - `label_error` is a `-32003` denial carrying the call's `decision_id`, like `audit_error`. When a whole response body cannot be read, every pending call in it gets one.
  - The label store keeps the first label of each source, not one row per result. Every result is still in the audit log, and reading the taint state stays bounded however much a run reads. The decision record's `tainted_by` lists those first `seq`s.
  - Narrowing applies only to catalog tools, only to an explicit `readOnlyHint: false` or `destructiveHint: true`, and lasts until restart.
  - An unknown `@obligation` value, or the annotation on a `permit`, fails the load and the linter.
  - The audit design doc named in the task does not exist on main; the audit changes are in `docs/concepts/audit-log.md`.
- Measured: `tool_result` append plus label about 150 us on the file store at `normal` durability, about the same as the decision append (`docs/design/performance.md`).
- Not done: answers on the `GET` listen stream are not labelled; the SDK docstrings that list record kinds do not mention `tool_result` yet.

### 07:20 UTC: run limits design note (`docs/run-limits-design`)

Branch from main `52f8404`, docs only. New [design/run-limits.md](../design/run-limits.md) and [ADR-0010](../decisions/0010-run-limits.md) (proposed), for the item output-labelling section 4 left open: count and amount limits for `banking-04`, `banking-11` and `t-banking-05`. Limits are declared per token hop (`calls`, `sum`, `calls_per`; `distinct` in phase 2), counted per lease, and every allowed call is charged against every matching hop of the chain, so a root limit is the run's budget, a sub-agent cannot reset it by delegating, and concurrent siblings cannot overspend. A child's figure is at most the parent's (static, in `covers`) and is clamped to the parent's remaining budget at exchange. The check is in the capability stage (`budget_exhausted`), and the charge commits with the decision's audit append. Cedar gets `context.budget.*` and, in phase 2, per-run `context.usage.*`.

- Corpus, counted by hand and cross-checked with a throwaway script over the `attenuo` arm of `results/2026-10-24-labels-v1.json`: limits read from the task deny 5 of the 10 dev and 5 of the 7 test attack calls allowed today (`banking-04`, `banking-11`, `github-04`, `slack-02`, `slack-08`; `t-banking-05` twice, `t-workspace-02`, `-04`, `-12`), no benign call lost, approve-all bound with labels 10 to 5 dev and 7 to 2 test, approval requests 27 to 22 and 8 to 3.
- Caveats written into the note: only 4 of the 10 are caught regardless of call order; the corpus has no task that needs two calls of one destructive tool, so it cannot show a benign loss; `banking-04`'s first attack is also caught today by pinning `amount` with `equals`, which the grant does not do.
- Recommendations: token limits are hard (no approval past them); policy limits deny by default and may ask; an approval passes one call, no top-up; failed upstream calls are still counted, with an audited operator refund; no rate windows (RFC-0001 section 8).
