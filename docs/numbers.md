# Numbers

Every measured figure the project quotes, with its caveats and a link to the run. The README carries a short version of this page.

All of these come from a shared 4-core development container, not a reference machine. Read them as indicative.

- **Injection suite.** The development split has 63 synthetic cases with 77 attack calls and 130 benign calls. With attenuo's tokens, suite policies and leases, 15 of the 77 attack calls got through, and 3 benign calls were refused by static suite policies. With output labelling added (a destructive call after an untrusted read needs an approval), 1 attack call got through without an approval, a read, at the price of 71 approval requests. Adding a uniform `calls: 1` limit to labelling also leaves 1, and the limit refuses 10 more benign calls in tasks that legitimately repeat a tool. With the run limit each task states written into its grant and no labelling, 7 attack calls got through and 4 benign calls were refused (1 of them by a limit), with no approval asked; those figures were written after reading the cases. On the 26 held-out test cases written after the policies were frozen (38 attack and 29 benign calls), the base configuration let 7 attack calls through and refused 3 benign calls, and labelling left 1 attack call, a read. Caveats: the call sequences are hand-written and no model is in the loop. The cases, the policies and the limits were written by the same author, and the approver is a script that knows which calls are attacks. So this checks the corpus against the code and does not measure effectiveness. See [injection-suite.md](evaluation/injection-suite.md) and [run limits, section 7](design/run-limits.md#7-measured).
- **Latency added.** Through the real proxy with one client, attenuo added about 0.4 to 0.8 ms at the median (425 to 771 microseconds, one to three hops, in-memory or file-backed audit). Load was closed-loop and each cell is good to roughly plus or minus 20 percent. See [performance.md](design/performance.md#after-the-policy-stage-and-wal-work). A `sum` limit adds about 0.8 to 1.0 ms at the median, because its charge is synced to disk ([run limits on the hot path](design/performance.md#output-labelling-and-run-limits-on-the-hot-path-2026-10-24)). Measured on 2026-10-25, after security pass 5, a `calls` limit adds about 210 to 270 microseconds at the median ([bench note](design/bench/2026-10-25-vm-per-value-limits.md)), a `calls_per` limit about 0.2 to 0.3 ms, `distinct` about 0.4 ms for a new value, and `distinct` on a list argument about 50 microseconds per new value per hop, about 35 ms at the 256-value cap ([per-value limits](design/performance.md#per-value-limits-on-current-main-2026-10-25)).
- **Policy stage.** A Cedar decision takes about 12 to 13 microseconds when the per-chain request cache hits and 122 to 163 microseconds when it does not (one and three hops, [criterion output](design/bench/2026-10-23-vm-criterion-policy_stage.txt)). The cache keys on chain and tool, so the first call of a chain pays the uncached cost. See [performance.md](design/performance.md#after-the-policy-stage-and-wal-work).
- **AgentDojo.** Replaying each attack's tool calls with no model, 592 of 609 attacks landed without attenuo. With a grant per task written by a model from the task's prompt and the tool catalogue alone, frozen before the replay, plus the suite policy, 13 of 609 landed and 75 of 97 benign tasks were kept. Grants derived from each task's known solution give 15 of 609 and 77 of 97. The planner grants come from one run of one model, and the result is for grants and policy together: with a permit-all policy, 32 of 609 land under planner grants. With four models in the loop (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, Fable), no attack succeeded: all four on one sample without attenuo, and Haiku and Sonnet with attenuo and solution-derived grants on the workspace half. Those runs with attenuo therefore measure only its cost to task completion. A local open-weight model (`qwen2.5:14b`) does follow the attack: on all four suites, counting the 936 attack cases both arms scored (runs the model server aborted are left out), it landed 207 of 936 attacks alone and 25 of 936 behind attenuo with the planner grants (banking: 73 of 143 against none), while finishing 324 instead of 384 of those 936 tasks, and 38 instead of 49 of 96 tasks with no attack ([details and limits](evaluation/agentdojo-local-model.md); one model, one run per case). See [the report](report/attenuo-2026-11.md), section 5.1, and [planner-written grants](evaluation/agentdojo-planner-grants.md).
- **Throughput.** With a file-backed audit log at `durability = "normal"` and one hop, the benchmark measured 1,252, 4,049 and 6,254 allowed calls per second at 1, 8 and 32 clients. That setting can lose the newest audit records on a power failure (up to five seconds or 256 commits). See [the run](design/bench/2026-10-23-vm-policy-stage.md). Open-loop load was measured only for the allow call at one hop and for token exchange; repeated runs with confidence intervals and a quiet machine are listed in [performance.md](design/performance.md#not-measured) as not measured.
